Reflections

Creating my CV in Markdown (STAR)

Situation: I wanted a clean, professional way to present my skills and experience online.

Task: Structure and format my CV in Markdown so it reads well and looks good on GitHub.

Action: I learned Markdown syntax, organised the sections clearly, and used headings, lists and links to highlight key information.

Result: A well-structured, accessible CV that shows my work effectively, and stronger documentation and formatting skills.

Mock video interview

I practised presenting myself in a recorded setting. I focused on speaking clearly, keeping good posture and organising my thoughts before answering. I felt nervous at first and grew more confident as I went on. I learned how much preparation and clear communication matter, and this will help me in future interviews and presentations.

GitHub Pages

GitHub Pages let me turn a repository into a live website and show my work more professionally. I organised my content, applied basic styling and checked that everything displayed correctly online. The setup was challenging at first, but I improved by experimenting and following the documentation. I now know how to publish projects on the web and feel more confident sharing my work publicly.

Tying it together

A good e-portfolio compiles evidence I chose myself, explains why each piece matters, and shows learning through reflection. Each page of this site follows that approach: the evidence shows what I can do, and these reflections show how I got there.
